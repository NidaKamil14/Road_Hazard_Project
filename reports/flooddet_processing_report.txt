5. BOUNDARY CLAMPING & COORDINATE CORRECTION
================================================================================
Procedure Applied to all Category 45 bounding boxes:
  1. clamped_x_min = max(0.0, min(x_min, image_width))
  2. clamped_y_min = max(0.0, min(y_min, image_height))
  3. clamped_x_max = max(0.0, min(x_min + width, image_width))
  4. clamped_y_max = max(0.0, min(y_min + height, image_height))
  5. new_width  = clamped_x_max - clamped_x_min
  6. new_height = clamped_y_max - clamped_y_min
  7. x_center = (clamped_x_min + new_width / 2.0) / image_width
  8. y_center = (clamped_y_min + new_height / 2.0) / image_height
  9. norm_width  = new_width / image_width
 10. norm_height = new_height / image_height
